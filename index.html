About

I am an Industrial Engineering student at Purdue. I grew up in the Bay Area as the son of a flight attendant and a consultant. As such, travel and aviation in general has been a part of my life since I was a child.

I have a deep passion for systems work, data analytics, and people. For the past two years, I've had the opportunity to work at both United Airlines and American Airlines, where I have been able to apply my skills in driving operational excellence at their busiest hubs.

Outside of work, I enjoy travel, aviation, reading, cooking, swimming, and photography.

I will return to American Airlines' Airport Operations team after graduation in 2027.
