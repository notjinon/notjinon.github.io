about me

hi! i'm jino, a fourth-year student at purdue university, majoring in industrial engineering with a minor in economics, a certificate in data science applications, and a lean six sigma green belt certification. i like analyzing data to make smart decisions with impact, which has led me to the high-stakes yet messy field of aviation.

at purdue, i explore the intersection of engineering and institutions, working with the department of political science on ML and big data projects that analyze legislative work, campaign finance, and electoral systems over time.

most recently, i worked with american airlines' airport performance analytics team out of fort worth, developing a performance scorecard for 12,000+ fleet service agents and analyzing staffing, leader, and peer effects across major stations.

before that, i worked with united airlines' transformation and process optimization team at newark liberty, leading analysis and improvements on station-wide processes. my major projects included a study of late-call cabin maintenance as a delay driver, biometric departure performance tracking for international flights, and migration of internal analytics reporting from spotfire to power bi.

on a more personal note, i'm an avid swimmer and traveler, a decent home chef, a tinkerer always thinking about their next project, and a curious reader who enjoys nonfiction literature.

my guilty pleasure, though, is gaming.

currently working on:

  • research on campaign finance patterns across state legislative elections
  • a lightweight, dynamic CYOA text-based browser game satirizing the internship recruitment process
  • serving as president of purdue filipino association
