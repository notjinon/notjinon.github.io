about me

hi! i'm jino, a sophomore at purdue university, majoring in industrial engineering with a minor in economics and a certificate in data science applications. i like analyzing data to make smart decisions with impact.

at purdue, i explore the intersection of engineering and institutions, working with our liberal arts collaboratory in applying ML and big data to analyze the legislative process and its evolution over time.

i've also led operations for my rocketry team, managing systems, growth, and development cycles to support agile iteration. similarly, through my work with the purdue filipino association, i focus on external collaboration and planning large-scale intercollegiate events.

outside of school, i'll soon be joining united airlines as an intern on their process optimization team at Newark Liberty.

on a more personal note, i'm an avid swimmer and traveler, a decent home chef, a tinkerer always thinking about their next project, and a curious reader who enjoys literature about history and politics.

my guilty pleasure, though, is gaming.

currently working on:

  • an SBERT-powered project to analyze legislative priorities since the 100th congress
  • a lightweight, text-based browser game satirizing the internship recruitment process
  • modeling the effects of nonpartisan primaries on donation behavior within state elections
